Core Content Literacy Engine

Three Central Pillars of Financial Content Literacy

Evaluating financial content requires checking what is claimed, why it is published, and who is saying it.

1. Claim Evidence-Checker

Extracts specific financial promises (e.g., "5% daily return", "multibagger in 3 days") and cross-checks them with realistic market benchmarks, historical indices, and SEBI return guidelines.

Key Check: Compares claims against risk-free rates & Nifty 50 historical CAGR.

2. Promotion vs Education Classifier

Identifies hidden affiliate links, paid tip subscriptions, sponsored calls, and pump-and-dump triggers versus genuine, neutral financial educational content.

Key Check: Flags scarcity bias, VIP channel invites & affiliate referral tokens.

3. Source Tracer

Traces origin signals of viral WhatsApp messages, Telegram channels, YouTube finfluencers, and checks stated registration against official SEBI / RBI directories.

Key Check: Verifies SEBI Registration (RA/IA/INZ) & valid intermediary handles.
